# CA Interview Preparation - Comprehensive Syllabus & Modules

This document outlines the curriculum for the IDAA project, from zero to basic, covering technical rigor, industry awareness, and behavioral excellence.

---

## 🏛️ Module 1: Technical Proficiency (Core Subjects)
*Focus: Mastery of standards and legislative frameworks.*

### 1.1 Financial Reporting (Ind AS / IFRS / AS)
- [ ] **Revenue Recognition (Ind AS 115)**: 5-step model, contracts, performance obligations.
- [ ] **Leases (Ind AS 116)**: ROU asset, lease liability, exemptions.
- [ ] **Financial Instruments (Ind AS 109)**: Classification, measurement, impairment.
- [ ] **Business Combinations**: Purchase price allocation, goodwill, NCI.
- [ ] **Provisions & Contingencies**: Ind AS 37, recognition criteria.

### 1.2 Audit & Assurance
- [ ] **Standards on Auditing (SAs)**: Materiality (SA 320), Risk Assessment (SA 315).
- [ ] **Internal Financial Controls (IFC)**: Design and operating effectiveness.
- [ ] **CARO 2020**: Reporting requirements for statutory auditors.
- [ ] **Professional Ethics**: Independence, confidentiality, code of conduct.

### 1.3 Taxation (Direct & Indirect)
- [ ] **Corporate Taxation**: Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT), deferred tax.
- [ ] **Transfer Pricing**: Arm's length price, methods, documentation.
- [ ] **GST**: Input Tax Credit (ITC), reverse charge, place of supply.
- [ ] **TDS/TCS**: Key sections and recent amendments.

### 1.4 Corporate Laws
- [ ] **Companies Act 2013**: Dividends, accounts, and audit sections.
- [ ] **SEBI Regulations**: Listing requirements for corporate governance.

## 📊 Module 2: Financial Management & Analysis
*Focus: Practical application of finance in business decision-making.*

### 2.1 Financial Statement Analysis
- [ ] **Ratio Analysis**: Liquidity, Solvency, Profitability, and Turnover.
- [ ] **Cash Flow Analysis**: Operating, Investing, and Financing activities.

### 2.2 Valuation & Modeling
- [ ] **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)**: FCF calculation, terminal value, WACC.
- [ ] **Relative Valuation**: P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/B multiples.

### 3.1 Macro-Economics
